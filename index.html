04 / WHO

Built by an owner, not a marketer.

Taran Aujla · Salesperson

I grew up managing and helping acquire my family’s multiplexes in Montreal, went on to invest in real estate, and spent close to a decade in real estate law before becoming a licensed Salesperson. I have lived in a plex, collected the rent and fixed what broke — so I read these buildings as homes and businesses, not just listings.

We build for what a multiplex is — not for show.

A multiplex is income-producing housing. It has to be structurally sound, presentable, and genuinely well built inside: quality materials, real soundproofing, and proper separation between units so every household lives well. That is where the money should go.

Too many builders in the GTA treat multiplexes like custom luxury homes — which only adds cost, not value. We keep projects affordable by spending on what matters (durability, function, livable homes) and skipping the expensive finishes that don’t earn their keep.
