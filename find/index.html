Why a multiplex

The math ordinary buyers are never shown.

Ontario's rules changed. In some wards a single lot can now hold up to six units, plus a garden suite where permitted. Handled right, the rents from the extra homes can carry much of the mortgage — so you live for far less, or hold it for cash flow. Every lot is different, and the numbers are only ever real once we prove them on the specific property. That is exactly what we do before you commit a dollar.
